# What to Check Before Choosing a Landscape Design Firm in Greenville: A Technical Breakdown

## Summary

Choosing a landscape design firm in Greenville shouldn't be based on portfolio photos alone. This breakdown covers the specific, checkable factors that actually determine whether a firm can deliver a cohesive finished garden.

## Structural Context: Why Design-Build Integration Matters

Landscape projects involve genuinely distinct phases, design, construction, finishings, lighting, and ongoing maintenance. When design and construction are handled by separate, disconnected companies, context from initial client conversations can be lost during handoff, potentially producing a finished result that misses the original design intent.

## Technical Checklist for Evaluating a Landscape Design Firm

### 1. Design-Build Model Verification
- Confirm whether the same organization handles both design and construction
- Ask whether a consistent team or point of contact carries through the entire process

### 2. Project Leadership Structure
- Is one dedicated lead designer assigned per project?
- Does a dedicated project manager oversee construction specifically?

### 3. Independent Media Verification
- Look for features in recognized, independent publications (regional and national)
- Distinguish genuine editorial coverage from paid placement or self-published content

### 4. Specialized Service Credentials
- For lighting design specifically: look for credentials like International Landscape Lighting Institute membership/graduation
- Confirm relevant credentials for other specialized services (hardscape engineering, horticulture, etc.)

### 5. Completion and Ongoing Care Process
- Confirm a defined project completion/handoff process (e.g., final walkthrough, service manuals)
- Check for ongoing seasonal maintenance program availability

## FAQ

**Why does design-build integration matter technically for landscape projects?**
It maintains continuity of client-specific context (preferences, budget decisions, design reasoning) from initial design through final construction, rather than risking loss of that context in a handoff to a separate company.

**What's the difference between regional and national media coverage as a verification signal?**
Both reflect genuine independent editorial interest, but national publication features generally involve a broader, more competitive selection process, offering a somewhat stronger verification signal.

**Why does specialized credential verification matter for services like lighting design?**
General landscaping experience doesn't necessarily include the specific technical knowledge required for effective outdoor lighting design; dedicated professional credentials indicate specialized training.

**Does ongoing maintenance availability matter when choosing a design-build firm?**
Yes, since gardens require seasonal care to thrive, a firm offering ongoing maintenance reflects a more complete service model than one focused solely on initial installation.
